What is Percentage Calculator?

Shop says 25% off $120 jacket. How much do you save? Student scored 45 out of 60, what percent? Percentage calculations appear daily in shopping, taxes, tips, grades, profit margins. Doing (part/whole*100) manually is error-prone. This calculator solves three common types instantly offline.

Formulas: X% of Y = X/100*Y. X is what % of Y = X/Y*100. Percent increase = (New-Old)/Old*100. All done locally via JavaScript, no data sent.

How to use

  1. For "What is 20% of 150?" enter 20 and 150, click Calculate → 30.
  2. For "30 is what % of 120?" enter 30 and 120 → 25%.

Real example

Jacket $120, 25% off → 0.25*120=30 savings, final $90. Exam 45/60 → 45/60*100=75%.

How it works

Simple arithmetic in JavaScript parseFloat. Rounding to 2 decimals. No external library.

When to use?

  • Shopping discounts
  • Grade percentages
  • Profit margin

When NOT to use?

For compound interest, use loan calculator, not simple percent.

Common errors

Entering 0.25 instead of 25 for 25% gives wrong result. Enter 25, not 0.25.

FAQ

Stores data? No.

How to calculate increase? Use (New-Old)/Old*100 manually or use discount calculator for decrease.
