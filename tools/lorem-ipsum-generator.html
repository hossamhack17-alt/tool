Lorem Ipsum Generator

Generate dummy text for design mockups. Paragraphs, sentences, words.

What is Lorem Ipsum Generator?

Designers need placeholder text to show client how layout looks without being distracted by real content. Lorem ipsum is standard dummy text since 1500s, derived from Cicero's writings. It looks like Latin but is scrambled, so readers focus on design, not meaning.

This generator creates paragraphs, sentences or words instantly offline. Used in Figma, wireframes, print layouts. No server, no tracking. You can generate 3 paragraphs for hero section, 50 words for card, etc.

How to use

  1. Choose type: paragraphs, sentences, words.
  2. Enter count.
  3. Click Generate, copy.

Real example

Paragraphs 2 → two paragraphs of lorem ipsum, each ~70 words. Words 20 → 20 words of dummy text for button label mockup.

How it works

Stores classic lorem paragraph in JS constant. For paragraphs, repeats full paragraph. For sentences, splits by ". " and cycles. For words, splits by space and cycles. O(n).

When to use?

  • Figma mockups
  • Website wireframes
  • Print layout samples
