What is Unit Price Calculator?

Supermarket sells 400g coffee for $4 and 1kg for $9. Which is cheaper? $4 looks cheaper, but per kg: 400g $4 → $10/kg, 1kg $9 → $9/kg, so 1kg is cheaper. Unit price calculator computes cost per single unit (per kg, liter, piece) to reveal true cheapest option.

Shoppers use it to save 15-20% monthly. Small businesses use it to price products. Runs locally, no data sent. Formula: unit price = total price / total quantity.

How to use

  1. Enter price and quantity for Product A (e.g., $4 and 400).
  2. Enter price and quantity for Product B (e.g., $9 and 1000).
  3. Click Compare → shows unit price and winner.

Real example

A: 500ml shampoo $5 → $10 per liter. B: 750ml $6.5 → $8.66 per liter → B cheaper by $1.33 per liter.

How it works

Unit = price / quantity. Compare unitA vs unitB. Difference = |unitA - unitB|. Rounding to 4 decimals for precision. O(1).

When to use?

  • Grocery shopping
  • Compare bulk vs small packs

FAQ

Handles different units? Ensure both quantities use same unit (both grams or both kg) before comparing.
