BMI Calculator

Calculate Body Mass Index. Informational only, not medical advice.

This is for information only, not medical advice. See Disclaimer.

What is BMI Calculator?

You are 70kg and 175cm tall. Is that normal? BMI (Body Mass Index) gives a quick screening number: weight divided by height squared. Doctors use it to categorize underweight, normal, overweight, obese. It is not a diagnosis, but a starting point to discuss with a healthcare professional.

This calculator runs locally in your browser using JavaScript. No weight or height is sent to any server. Your health data stays private. Formula used is BMI = kg / (m)², same as NHS and CDC. We also show category thresholds: Underweight <18.5, Normal 18.5–24.9, Overweight 25–29.9, Obese ≥30, per CDC guidelines.

How to use

  1. Enter weight in kilograms (e.g., 70).
  2. Enter height in centimeters (e.g., 175).
  3. Click Calculate BMI. Result shows BMI value and category.

Real example

Weight 70kg, height 175cm → height in meters 1.75 → 1.75² = 3.0625 → BMI = 70 / 3.0625 = 22.9 → Normal weight.

Another: 90kg, 170cm → 1.7² = 2.89 → 90/2.89 = 31.1 → Obese category.

How it works

Formula: BMI = weight(kg) / [height(m) * height(m)]. Height converted from cm to m by dividing by 100. Rounding to 1 decimal. No other factors. Source: CDC https://www.cdc.gov/bmi/about/ and NHS https://www.nhs.uk/live-well/healthy-weight. This is informational, not medical advice.

When to use?

  • Quick self-screening before doctor visit.
  • Tracking weight change over months.
  • School health education.

When NOT to use?

BMI does not account for muscle mass, so athletes may show overweight despite low fat. It does not account for pregnancy, age, sex, or medical conditions. Do not use as sole diagnosis. Consult doctor. See Disclaimer.

Common errors

1. Using pounds/inches: This calculator expects kg and cm. Convert: kg = lbs /2.2046, cm = inches *2.54.

2. Zero height: Returns error — we check and hide result if invalid.

FAQ

Is BMI accurate? It is screening, not diagnostic. Doctor may use waist circumference, body fat.

Does it store my data? No, calculation is local via JavaScript, no storage.

What is healthy BMI? 18.5–24.9 per CDC, but individual health varies.

Source? CDC and NHS as linked above.
