What is Loan EMI Calculator?

You want to borrow $10,000 at 8% annual for 5 years. How much will you pay monthly? Loan EMI calculator computes Equated Monthly Installment using standard amortization formula. Banks use same formula, but may add fees, insurance, variable rates that this simple calculator does not include.

Runs locally in browser, no financial data sent. Formula: EMI = P * R * (1+R)^N / ((1+R)^N -1) where P principal, R monthly rate = annual/12/100, N months = years*12. Source: Investopedia loan amortization.

How to use

  1. Enter amount, annual rate %, years.
  2. Click Calculate EMI.
  3. See monthly, total interest, total payment.

Real example

P=10000, annual 8% → monthly 0.006666, N=60 → EMI = 10000*0.006666*1.4898/(0.4898) ≈ $202.76. Total payment 202.76*60=12165.6, interest 2165.6.

How it works

JavaScript Math.pow for (1+R)^N. Rounding to 2 decimals. Does not include processing fees, taxes, early repayment penalties. Estimate only.

When to use / NOT

Use for budgeting, comparing loans. NOT for final bank decision — consult bank for exact schedule including fees.

Common errors

Entering monthly rate instead of annual gives tiny EMI. Ensure annual.
