What is Discount Calculator?

Store says $120 jacket 25% off, what do you pay? Discount calculator computes savings = original * discount% /100 and final = original - savings. Used by shoppers, small business owners setting sale prices, and students learning percentages.

Runs locally, no data sent. Formula: savings = price * discount/100, final = price - savings. Educational only, not financial advice.

How to use

  1. Enter original price (e.g., 120).
  2. Enter discount % (e.g., 25).
  3. Click Calculate → savings $30, final $90.

Real example

$200 shoes 15% off → savings 30, final 170.

How it works

parseFloat for price and discount, multiply, subtract, toFixed(2). O(1).

When NOT to use?

Does not include tax. Final price is before tax. For tax inclusive, add tax after discount.
