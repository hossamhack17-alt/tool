Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026 — Effective for https://israelpost.cc

1. Overview

IsraelPost Tools (israelpost.cc) provides browser-based tools. We designed tools to run locally in your browser so we do NOT receive your file contents. This policy explains what we do collect, what third parties collect, and your rights.

2. What we collect and what we do NOT collect

Tool inputs — NOT collected: When you paste CSV, JSON, text, or select a file for hash verification, that data is processed in your browser memory via JavaScript (split, JSON.parse, FileReader, SubtleCrypto). It is NOT sent to our server. You can verify by opening DevTools → Network tab → no POST of file content occurs. You can also disconnect internet after page loads and tools still work, proving local processing.

Contact form — collected when you submit: If you use contact form, we use FormSubmit.co to deliver email to admin@israelpost.cc. We receive your email address and message content to reply. Stored in email inbox only, retained until issue resolved, then archived. You can request deletion.

Server logs: Hosting is Cloudflare Pages. Cloudflare may log IP address, user-agent, and timestamp for security, DDoS protection and rate limiting. We do not access these logs personally beyond Cloudflare dashboard.

Analytics (optional): We may use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which is privacy-first, does not use cookies, does not track across sites. It shows which tools are popular, country-level stats, without personal identification.

3. Cookies

We do not set cookies ourselves for tools. If you see ads from Google AdSense, Google may use cookies to serve personalized or non-personalized ads per Google Ads Policy and Google Cookie Policy. You can opt out of personalized ads via Google Ad Settings (adssettings.google.com). Users in EEA may see consent prompt for cookies.

4. Third parties

  • cdn.tailwindcss.com: Serves Tailwind CSS framework, static asset, no personal data.
  • cdnjs.cloudflare.com: Serves qrcodejs library for QR generator, static asset.
  • formsubmit.co: Contact form delivery to admin@israelpost.cc, receives email and message you submit.
  • Google AdSense: When active, may collect device information, IP, use cookies for ad personalization and measurement. See Google's privacy policy. We place AdSense code but do not control Google's data collection beyond placement.
  • Cloudflare: Hosting and security, may log IP for protection.

5. Data processing location

Tool inputs: Processed locally in your browser, not transmitted, not stored on server, deleted when you close tab (memory cleared).

Contact messages: Transmitted via HTTPS to FormSubmit, then to our email provider, stored in inbox.

6. Your rights

You can request deletion of any message you sent to admin@israelpost.cc by emailing same address. Since tool inputs are never stored on server, there is nothing to delete for tool usage.

7. Children's privacy

Site is general audience, not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect children's data. Tool inputs are local, contact form requires email.

8. Changes to policy

If we add analytics or change third parties, we update this policy and update Last updated date.

9. Contact

For privacy questions: admin@israelpost.cc
